Images and video for the Nexus page, captured by the author on their own PC. Nothing here was recorded or uploaded by
the agent. The video can be made later with the **showcase-video** skill (universal-modder:showcase-video: records the
game window with only the game's audio, picks moments from contact sheets, cuts a 20-45 s clip with one-line titles
and credits).

## Never use

- Activision, Infinity Ward or Call of Duty / Modern Warfare logos, box art, key art, wallpapers, store capsules or
  marketing renders, and FromSoftware / Bandai Namco / Elden Ring logos or key art.
- Screenshots or footage of MW2 itself (its menus, maps or HUD outside Elden Ring), trailers, or anyone else's footage
  (unless they agreed and are credited by name).
- Copyrighted music in the video: use the game audio only, or music you have the rights to.
- Online play: nothing that looks like official online multiplayer (no invasions, no other players' names except your
  co-op friend with permission).
- Personal data: Windows user name, folder paths with your name, Steam names or ids, the co-op password, the Seamless
  `ersc_settings.ini`. Crop or blur the setup's install-folder row if it shows your user name.
- Text on images that uses "Call of Duty" as the mod's name. "Requires Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2 (2009)" is fine.
